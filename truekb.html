Privacy Policy

TrueKB: Photo Resize & Compress

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

⚡ Quick Summary for Google Play & Users

TrueKB is a completely offline, on-device utility application. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, upload, or share any personal images, documents, biometric information, or device data. All compression, resizing, and format conversions execute 100% locally on your device's processor.

🔒 1. Information Collection & Storage

TrueKB is engineered around a zero-knowledge architecture:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal contact details (email, phone number, name) to use any feature of TrueKB.
  • No Image Uploads: Photos, signatures, and documents you select for compression or resizing remain strictly on your physical device. No image data is ever sent to external cloud servers, third-party APIs, or analytics databases.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application does not contain behavioral trackers, device fingerprinting, or user telemetry SDKs.

📁 2. Device Permissions & Scoped Storage

TrueKB strictly adheres to Google Play's Least Privilege Access principles:

  • Android 10 and Higher (Scoped Storage): The application utilizes the modern Android System Photo Picker and MediaStore API. TrueKB requires zero runtime permissions to read selected photos or save compressed files to your public Pictures/TrueKB directory.
  • Android 9 and Lower (Legacy): The app requests WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (maxSdkVersion 28) exclusively to write your exported, resized photos into your device's Pictures folder.
  • Temporary Cache Files: Any intermediate scratch bitmaps generated during processing are automatically purged from the application's internal private cache directory upon session completion.

🛡️ 3. EXIF Metadata Sanitization

When you select the Strip EXIF privacy option (enabled by default), TrueKB scrubs sensitive embedded metadata from your JPEG files before saving, including:

  • GPS geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude)
  • Camera make, model, and lens serial numbers
  • Timestamps and hardware metadata

This ensures that photos uploaded to government portals or public exam websites do not inadvertently disclose your home or workplace location.

🔄 4. Third-Party Services & Google Play Core

TrueKB integrates the official Google Play In-App Update API (com.google.android.play.core). This service is provided directly by Google to detect critical app updates and security patches published on the Google Play Store.

Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how Google Play handles update checks.

👶 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TrueKB does not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted from any user, TrueKB complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

📬 6. Security & Contact Information

TrueKB includes enterprise-grade anti-tampering guards to protect users from modified or malware-injected clones distributed outside official channels.
